# Playback Tracking

## Overview

During playback, a vertical cursor line moves across the score to indicate the current playback position.

## Cursor appearance

A thin vertical line spanning all staves in the current system (from the top of the highest staff to the bottom of the lowest). Distinct from the note-entry cursor — different colour and always full-height across all parts.

## Auto-scroll

The view scrolls vertically to keep the playback cursor visible as playback advances through the score.

## After playback ends

- **Natural stop** (end of score reached): cursor is hidden.
- **Manual stop** (user presses stop): cursor remains at the stopped position.

## Interaction during playback

No note entry or editing interactions are available during playback (existing behaviour unchanged).
